Blinkr

A window that flashes a color on an interval. No countdown, no sound, and no clock. This page is a mockup for review: the window below and every settings panel on it are live.

While running, the window is blank. Move the pointer over it, click it, or press Tab to show the bar. It fades after about 2 seconds, when the pointer leaves, and never while one of its buttons has keyboard focus. This demo flashes every 5 seconds so you can see it work. The real app flashes on your interval and never shows a countdown.

Window states

The window never shows the time left, a clock, or a countdown. The only thing on it is a small control bar, and only when there is something to press.

With reduced motion on, the bar appears and disappears without fading. A click on the empty window while running only shows the bar.

Reaching settings

Four ways in. The gear is always in the bar. Space starts and stops. Escape or the close button closes settings.

Clicking the empty window never opens settings. Clicking it while settings are open does nothing. Only Escape or the close button closes them.

Flash states

A flash fills the whole window with the flash color over the idle color. Intensity sets the peak opacity and rate sets the period. Each frame shows the peak. Play shows the motion once.

Settings panel

A panel slides in from the right and leaves the left of the window visible, so Preview flash can be judged. Start hides the panel. In a short window the controls scroll and the two buttons stay pinned at the bottom. Every control below is live.

Choices to confirm

Design decisions the brief left open. Each one is easy to change now.

  • Panel is a right drawer, 380 pixels wide. The left of the window stays visible for previews. Below 560 pixels wide it fills the window.
  • The window has a small control bar, a translucent dark pill at the bottom centre. Stopped, it holds Start and a gear. Running, it holds Stop and the gear. Start and Stop are text, the gear is an icon named Settings.
  • Shortcuts live in tooltips, such as Start (Space), Stop (Space) and Settings (S). The faint hint text is gone.
  • While running, the window is blank until you move the pointer over it, click it, or press Tab. The bar then fades out after about 2 seconds without pointer movement, when the pointer leaves the window, and never while one of its buttons has keyboard focus. It does not wait for the pointer to leave the bar itself.
  • Starting from the bar hides it at once, unless the button was pressed from the keyboard. Moving the pointer brings it back.
  • A click on the empty window no longer opens settings. Running, it only shows the bar. The gear, S and the right-click menu open settings.
  • The bar is hidden while settings are open, and returns when they close. The panel keeps its own Start and Stop button. Only Escape or the close button closes settings.
  • The bar is dark and neutral, never amber, with a thin light edge so it stays visible over a very dark or very light idle color. Light controls, quiet by design, so the flash color stays the main color.
  • Reduced motion removes the fade. The bar shows and hides at once.
  • The right-click menu has two items, Settings and Start or Stop. It is drawn inside the window, not by the system.
  • S and Space work only when the window has focus and you are not typing in a field or on a button. Space on a focused bar button presses it.
  • Start hides the panel. If you open settings while running, the button says Stop and the panel stays until you close it.
  • Whole pulses fit the duration. The app plays a whole number of pulses and stretches the period to fit, so it is never faster than the rate you set. A 2 second flash at 0.25 per second is one 2 second pulse.
  • Color order carries on across flashes. With 3 colors and 2 pulses per flash, the next flash starts on the third color.
  • The warning is always visible and cannot be dismissed. It escalates at rate above 2 per second, or hard blink above 70%.
  • Start is disabled when interval or duration is invalid. Stop is never disabled. Preview flash is disabled only for an invalid duration.
  • Not validated: a flash duration longer than the interval. Say if it should be.
  • Intensity moves in steps of 5%, and the interval accepts decimals such as 1.5 minutes.
  • The panel is light on the dark window, and its controls are ink, not amber, so the flash color is the only color in the product.
