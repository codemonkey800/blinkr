Window controls

Start / Stop and Settings as one compact pill, centered in the Blinkr window, with a dark settings drawer. Nothing here is built yet.

Frames show the content area only. The title bar is drawn above it and is not counted in 760 × 480, 360 × 480 or 320 × 240. Frames are scaled down so they sit side by side. The live demo is at full size.

Direction: the compact pill, with a dark drawer

Review round 1 chose the compact pill (the earlier Option A) and a dark settings drawer. The other option is removed. Everything below is drawn around these two choices.

Still to confirm:

  • Placement. The pill is centered horizontally and vertically in the content area, in every state, at every size.
  • Styling. Dark pill with a light ring, a play or stop glyph beside the label, and 44px hit areas.
  • Drawer. Dark theme, replacing the light drawer in the earlier mockup. Layout, copy and behavior are unchanged.
  • Interaction. All current behavior is kept. One change is proposed below as P1 and needs a yes or no.

Current layout and the new one

The current bar sits 24px above the bottom. The pill replaces it, centered on the window.

The pill

One pill holding Start / Stop and a gear. The same shape as today, larger and centered.

  • Group: 164 × 54. Start / Stop is 100 × 44 with a play or stop glyph and its label. Gear is 44 × 44.
  • Start and Stop share one fixed slot, so switching between them moves nothing.
  • Settings has no visible text. Its name is "Settings", set for screen readers and as a hover tooltip showing the key S.
  • Dark pill, light ring. Reads over dark and light idle colors.

Anchor check

The dashed lines cross the center of the window. Start and Stop occupy the same box, so the pill does not move when the timer starts.

Stopped

Start and Settings are visible at the center. This is the state the app opens in.

Running, controls revealed

Pointer movement, a click on the window, or Tab brings the controls back. Stop and Settings sit in the same place as Start and Settings did.

Running, idle

A blank window. The controls are fully transparent and ignore the pointer, but Tab still reaches them.

Settings drawer open

The drawer is dark. The window controls are removed while it is open. At 760 wide the center of the window falls on the drawer's left edge, which is why they cannot stay. The drawer keeps its own Start / Stop and a close button. At 360 and 320 wide the drawer fills the window.

Over a light idle color

The drawer stays dark whatever the idle color is. A light rim and a shadow on its left edge keep it separate from a light window.

Hover and keyboard focus

Hover lightens the control. Keyboard focus draws a white ring. Tooltips are the system's native title tooltips, drawn here only to show the names and keys.

Reduced motion

With reduced motion on, the controls appear and disappear without a fade, and the drawer appears and leaves without a slide. Nothing else changes.

DefaultThe controls fade out over 250 ms.

Reduced motionThe controls are gone at once.

Interaction

Everything below is how the app behaves today. The pill keeps it.

Situation What happens
Stopped Controls stay visible.
Start pressed Controls hide at once, unless keyboard focus is on them. Then they stay.
Running Pointer movement, a click on the window, or Tab shows them. They fade after 2 seconds with no activity.
Keyboard focus on a control Controls stay visible until focus leaves.
Keys Space starts or stops. S opens settings. Esc closes settings.
Right-click The context menu opens as it does today.
Always No countdown, no time readout, no sound.

Proposed change P1: bring the controls back after the drawer finishes closing

Today the controls return the moment the drawer starts to close. With the controls at the center, they would pop in under the drawer's left edge while it slides away. P1 keeps them hidden until the slide ends, then fades them in. With reduced motion they return at once. This is a small change to when the controls show, not to what they do. Recommended: yes. If you say no, the controls return as soon as the drawer starts to close.

Worth knowing

  • The controls now appear where the pointer often rests. A click right after moving the mouse to the middle can land on Start or Stop. No guard is proposed, because the behavior matches today.
  • Hidden controls ignore the pointer, so a click on a hidden Stop shows the controls and does not stop the timer.

Try it

Click the window first. Move the pointer, press Space, S or Esc, or press Tab (Option + Tab in Safari). This demo uses the proposed behavior, including P1 and the dark drawer.
